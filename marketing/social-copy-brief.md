## 2. The big idea (why now)

- Customers used to Google you and get 10 links. Now many ask ChatGPT (or Gemini, Claude, Perplexity) and get **one answer with a few names**.
- If you're not one of those names, you don't lose a ranking. **You don't exist for that customer.** There's no click and nothing in your analytics. You just never came up.
- SEO agencies are still optimizing for the 10 blue links. Almost nobody is working on the answer.
- Alphaa does that work for you, as an agent, not a dashboard.

## 3. Who it's for

**Primary:** local business owners in the US and Canada. They're 35–60, non-technical and time-poor, and they buy outcomes, not tools.
- Dentists, med spas, chiropractors, physical therapists
- HVAC, plumbers, electricians, contractors, landscapers, cleaners
- Lawyers (personal injury, family, estate), accountants, insurance agents
- Restaurants, salons, real estate agents, auto repair

**Secondary:** small SaaS and online businesses that want to be cited by AI.

## 5. What Alphaa actually does (features)

**It checks what AI says about you**
- Asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity the questions your customers actually ask, live, and shows you the real answers.
- Shows who they recommended instead of you, and why: what those businesses have that you don't.
- Re-asks every week and tells you exactly what changed.

**It does the work to get you named**
- Writes and publishes the pages, FAQs and blog posts AI engines quote.
- Fixes the facts AI gets wrong or can't find (hours, services, locations).
- Adds the behind-the-scenes code AI reads (structured data, llms.txt). You never touch it.
- Keeps your Google Business Profile active with regular posts.
- Drafts replies to your reviews and watches reviews on other sites.
- Watches what your competitors publish.

**It works like a conversation, not a dashboard**
- It talks to you in plain English: "I asked ChatGPT about you this morning. Here's what it said, and here's what I'll fix."
- Anything public gets **one-tap approval** from you first: Post it / Edit / Skip.
- **Undo anytime.** Everything it publishes can be rolled back.
- A short weekly email is all you have to read.

**It connects to your website**
- WordPress (plugin) today. Webflow and Shopify apps are in review, so say "coming soon".

## 9. Voice and tone

- **Plain, calm, confident.** Like a smart friend who did the homework.
- **Short sentences.** One idea per line.
- **First-person agent voice works well:** "I asked ChatGPT about you this morning…"
- **Outcomes, not jargon.** Say "show up when customers ask AI", not "AEO / GEO / schema / llms.txt" (jargon is OK only on LinkedIn posts aimed at marketers).
- **Honest.** Admit what can't be promised. That honesty is the brand.
- Social post rules: **1 image, a 4–5 word headline on the image, a scroll-stopping hook, real value, simple, "get it in 2 seconds".**

**Visual style:** black and white only (Grok-style). Pure black background, white type, grey secondary text, no colour accents.

## 10. Never say (hard rules)

- ❌ "#1", "best", "leading", "top-rated" about Alphaa
- ❌ Promised rankings or timelines ("results in weeks", "guaranteed #1 on ChatGPT")
- ❌ Invented stats, customer counts, testimonials or case studies (no "1,200+ businesses", no "Miami law firm got 14 leads")
- ❌ "No signup" (the check asks for an email); "free trial" (there isn't one)
- ❌ Copilot or Google AI Overviews as engines we check. We check exactly **ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity**.
- ❌ Shopify or Webflow as live today (say "coming soon")
- ✅ Example scenarios are fine if labelled as examples ("Here's what a typical AI check looks like").
