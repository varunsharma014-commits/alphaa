## Rules every ad follows

- **Lead with getting customers from AI.** The hook is always: customers ask ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Perplexity who to hire, and it isn't naming you. Price, agency comparison and the guarantee live in body copy, the offer line, or retargeting. The "money gap" creative (c15) is **retargeting only**.
- Image headline 4 to 5 words, self-contained, names AI/ChatGPT. Black and white only.
- No em-dashes, no "#1/best/leading", no promised rankings or timelines, no invented stats/customers. Every chat answer on a creative carries an **Example** tag and blurred business names.
- Four AIs only: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity. Never "free trial" or "no signup".
- Guarantee wording, exactly: "If no AI names you in 90 days, your next month is free" + "Terms apply." 7-day refund. Month to month.
- Meta policy: industry callouts are written as "Dentists:" / "For dental practices", never "Are you a dentist?". No before/after or guaranteed-result claims.

|---|---|
| Prospecting | ~$700/mo (~$23/day). One campaign, one broad ad set: US + Canada, age 30 to 65, Advantage+ audience, no interest stacks (creative is the targeting). Advantage+ placements, exclude Audience Network. |
| Retargeting | ~$300/mo (~$10/day). Separate campaign, audiences below. Frequency cap watch: if 7-day frequency > 4, add creative or trim budget. |
| Optimization event | `Lead`. In code it fires on `/start` when the visitor enters their email to get the report (`StartAgent.tsx`, same moment as GA4 `scan_completed`) and on submit of the legacy `/scan` form (the `/for/` pages use that one). |
| Other pixel events in code | `ViewContent` (scan results), `InitiateCheckout` (Start today, value 99), `StartTrial` (dashboard `?upgraded=true`, i.e. paid). Use `StartTrial` as the purchase signal for check-to-paid reporting. |
| Destination | Every ad goes to `https://alphaa.app/start` with UTMs: `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=prospecting (or retargeting)&utm_content={ad id}_{concept}`. |

| Audience | Definition | Ads |
|---|---|---|

## Testing plan

**What we measure:** cost per completed check (`Lead`) and check-to-paid within 14 days (`StartTrial`). Not CPC, not likes. CAC target is $300 or less.

**Launch (week 1):** prospecting runs P01, P02, P03, P04, P08 (one ad per concept, both `-sq` and `-st` assets in the same ad so Meta places them). Retargeting runs R01, R02, R04, R05.

**Every week after:** ship 3 to 5 new creatives into prospecting. Rotate in P05, P06, P07 and the remaining callouts (P09 to P12) first, then new hooks from the best organic posts of the week ("Are you in the answer?" episodes, agent diary, 60-second fix). R03 and R06 join retargeting in week 2.

**Kill rule (from the playbook):** after about **$30 spend**, a creative with a weak thumb-stop rate is off.

| Signal | Video / Reels | Static image | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thumb-stop | 3-second views / impressions below ~25% | Link CTR below ~0.8% | Off at ~$30 spend |
| Cost per check | More than 2x the account average after ~$60 | same | Off |
| Winner | Cost per check at or below average with 3+ checks | same | Keep; duplicate the hook into a new format (static to video, square to story) |

Starting thresholds are guesses; reset them from your own week 1 to 2 baseline. Fix the first line or the image headline before touching audiences. Move budget to the 2 to 3 winners from day 31 and keep shipping 3 new a week.

**Later:** UGC-style ads only once real customers send a real clip of "ChatGPT named us". Never fake it. Founder video (see `video-ad-scripts.md`) goes in as soon as it's recorded; founder ads tend to earn trust on cold traffic.
