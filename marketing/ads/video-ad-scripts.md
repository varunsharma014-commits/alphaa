# Video ad scripts (Reels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts)

Four short scripts for Meta prospecting (and TikTok Spark Ads / YouTube Shorts from the test budget). All are 9:16, 1080x1920, captions burned in.

**Rules for every video**
- The first 2 seconds carry the hook: AI is naming other businesses, not you. No logo intro, no price in the hook.
- Black and white on-screen text: white type on black, grey secondary. Keep text out of the top 250px and bottom 340px (Reels/Stories UI).
- Any AI answer shown on screen is either a **real, unedited** answer from a check you ran (say so) or labelled **"Example"**. Blur every business name that isn't the viewer's (we never shame named small businesses).
- Four AIs only: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity. No "#1", no "best", no promised rankings or timelines, no invented results. No "free trial", no "no signup".
- Guarantee wording, if used: "If no AI names you in 90 days, your next month is free. Terms apply."
- End card on every video: **"See what AI says about you. alphaa.app/start"**. CTA button: Learn More.
- UTMs: same as the static ads, with `utm_content=v01_phone_demo`, `v02_founder`, `v03_split_screen`, `v04_agent_pov`.

## Production notes

- Record V01 and V03 from **real** checks you run the day you film; keep the raw recordings as proof. If the real answer happens to name the business you search for, pick another query. Never stage an answer.
- Burn captions in (most people watch muted). Font: system sans, white on a black box, 2 lines max.
- Export 1080x1920, under 30s. Make a 1:1 crop of V01 and V03 for Feed.
- Kill rule applies to video too: 3-second view rate weak after ~$30 spend means off. Rewrite the first line, not the whole video.
