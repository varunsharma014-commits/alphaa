04 · First 2 seconds

Hooks that work in 2026

Feeds are flooded with polished AI-generated content, so polish no longer stops the scroll. What stops it is specific, personal, provable: a real screen, a real question, the viewer's own situation. Seven hook families, each with a template.

1 · The self-test

"Open ChatGPT. Type 'best [your trade] in [your city]'. Are you in the answer?"

Turns a scroll into an action. The viewer runs the experiment and feels the problem themselves.

2 · Screen-proof

"I asked ChatGPT for a dentist in Austin. Watch who it picked."

A live screen recording of a real AI answer beats any claim. Real, unedited, on a phone.

3 · The contrarian truth

"Your SEO agency is optimizing for a page fewer of your customers ever see."

Picks a fight with something owners already resent. Variants: "SEO isn't dead. It just isn't where your customers are anymore."

4 · The lost customer

"A customer asked ChatGPT who to call this morning. It named your competitor."

Leads with the outcome we sell: customers from AI. Price never leads a hook; agency-fee math makes us look like a cheaper SEO agency. Keep $99 vs ~$2,000 for body copy, offer posts and retargeting.

5 · The agent diary

"My AI agent found out ChatGPT thinks we close at 5. We close at 8."

First-person, small and specific. Shows the product without selling it. Label it as an example when it is one.

6 · The insider secret

"3 things ChatGPT checks before it recommends a local business."

Save-bait. Gives real value and makes us the authority.

7 · The callout

"Plumbers: this one question decides if AI sends you jobs."

Industry name in the first word. Narrow beats broad: the right person stops; everyone else scrolls on, which is fine.

Hooks to retire

  • "AI is changing everything…" (everyone says it; nobody stops)
  • Agency-fee or "what does your SEO guy do" hooks (they put us in the SEO category)
  • "Did you know…?" and other quiz openers
  • Stock-photo or obviously AI-generated imagery
  • Any claim we can't show on screen

07 · Funnel

The acquisition engine

Attention→Free AI check→The result moment→Email→Nurture + retarget→$99 / month→First win→Share + refer

Everything points at one door: alphaa.app/start. The result moment ("ChatGPT named three businesses. You weren't one.") is our best salesperson. Protect it, test it, make it screenshot-able.

Channels, ranked for us

  1. Meta ads → free check. Owners live on Facebook and Instagram. Cheapest path to volume.
  2. Google search ads. High intent: "seo agency alternative", "how to show up on chatgpt", "ai seo for [trade]".
  3. Founder-led LinkedIn. Free, compounding, and where lawyers and multi-location owners are.
  4. Short video (organic). Lottery-ticket reach; one hit can deliver weeks of checks.
  5. Partnerships. Web designers and small agencies who build sites for local businesses: they refer, we pay recurring commission. Also vertical newsletters, associations, chambers.
  6. Product-led loops. Customers share their "ChatGPT now names us" win (share pages already exist). Ask for a referral right after the first win.
  7. AI search itself. The daily blog engine and YouTube audits so AI names Alphaa when owners ask about this problem.

Outbound (light, targeted)

Run the check on a prospect's business first, then send them their own result: "I asked ChatGPT about you. Here's what it said." Personal, relevant, 3 sentences. Use the separate outreach domain (tryalphaa.com) once warmed, never alphaa.app.

08 · Paid

Paid ads

Rule one: creative is the targeting. On Meta in 2026, broad audiences plus a strong creative beat narrow interest stacks. Our job is to ship new creative every week and let the algorithm find the owner.

ChannelBudgetSetup
Meta prospecting~$700/moBroad US + Canada, 30–65. Optimize for completed checks. 3–5 new creatives a week; kill losers at 3 days.
Meta retargeting~$300/moPeople who started but didn't finish a check, and checkers who didn't buy. Show the guarantee and the agent at work.
Google search~$800/moExact and phrase match on intent terms. Send to /start or the matching /for/[industry] page.
Test budget~$200/moTikTok Spark Ads boosting organic winners; YouTube in-stream later.

Ad creative that should win

  • The phone-screen demo: someone types "best dentist near me" into ChatGPT; the answer appears; overlay "Is your practice here?" CTA: Check yours free.
  • The founder ad: Varun, 20 seconds, "I built an AI agent because…" Founder ads earn trust on cold traffic.
  • The split screen: left, a cluttered Google results page; right, ChatGPT's single answer with three names. Overlay: "Which one are your customers using?" Great as a Short or Reel.
  • The offer card (retargeting only): black card, white type, "$99 a month. Month to month." plus the guarantee. Never a cold-traffic hook.
  • The callout: "HVAC owners:" + the self-test. One version per top industry.
  • UGC-style: once real customers exist, their own phone clip of "ChatGPT named us." Never fake this.

Measure cost per completed check and check → paid, not cost per click. Kill rule: a creative with a weak thumb-stop (3-second view) rate after ~$30 spend is off.

09 · Nurture

Email nurture

Most people who run the check won't buy that day. Email turns the result moment into a 14-day conversation. Write it from the agent, in plain text, short.

Must-have before sending to non-customers: one-click unsubscribe, a mailing address in the footer, and consent wording at the email field (CASL for Canada). The copy is drafted in email-flows.md; the infra is the blocker.

Behaviour triggers

  • Visited pricing but didn't buy → same-day "questions?" email from Varun.
  • Started checkout but didn't finish → 1 hour later, the guarantee + 7-day refund.
  • Ran a second check → they're interested; send the agent-diary story.

Customer emails (already partly live)

  • Weekly briefing: what changed, what the agent did, what needs approval. Example tone: "I asked ChatGPT about you today. I fixed your hours and drafted three review replies. Two need your OK." Visible weekly work is what keeps people who left agencies over opaque reporting.
  • First-win alert: "Perplexity named you today." With a share button.
  • Referral ask: sent only after a first win, never before.

13 · Non-negotiable

Guardrails

  • Never fake proof. No invented customers, results, testimonials or counts. Example scenarios are labelled as examples. This is the fastest way to lose the honesty edge.
  • No "#1", no promised rankings. We promise the work and the guarantee, not the outcome.
  • Four AIs, named exactly: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity.
  • Don't shame named small businesses in public audits. Show industry patterns, blur names.
  • Email law first: unsubscribe and consent before any marketing email to non-customers.
  • Plain language beats legal language in posts ("tap approve"), as long as it stays true.
