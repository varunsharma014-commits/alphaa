## Quora (Varun personal, disclosure at the end)

### 1. How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?
- URL: https://www.quora.com/How-do-I-get-my-business-recommended-by-ChatGPT
- Draft:
> Start by checking what it says today. Ask ChatGPT the way a customer would ("best family dentist in [town]", "emergency plumber near [neighborhood]") a few different ways, and note who it names and why.
>
> Then work on what it reads. It leans on your website, your Google Business Profile, review sites and local directories, so make sure your name, address, phone, hours and services match everywhere. Put your services, areas and prices (or ranges) in plain text on the site, and answer the questions customers actually ask on their own pages. Ask happy customers to mention the specific service in their reviews, since "fixed our tankless water heater same day" says more than "great service".
>
> OpenAI also just started letting owners claim their business listing inside ChatGPT, so claim it and check the details are right.
>
> There is no form or fee that gets you recommended. Being the clearest, most consistent answer is the whole game.
>
> (I build Alphaa, which does this for local businesses, so I'm biased.)

### 2. Why are brands invisible in ChatGPT even when they rank on Google?
- URL: https://www.quora.com/Why-are-brands-invisible-in-ChatGPT-even-when-they-rank-on-Google
- Draft:
> Because ranking and being recommended are different jobs. Google ranks pages for a query. ChatGPT reads several sources and writes one answer with two or three names in it, so it favors businesses it can describe confidently from more than one place.
>
> The usual gaps: the site ranks but never says plainly what you do, where, and for whom; the brand is barely mentioned anywhere else (reviews, local press, directories, industry sites); details conflict between sources; or the site blocks AI crawlers without anyone realizing.
>
> A quick test: ask ChatGPT "what do you know about [your business]?" If the answer is vague or wrong, that is the problem to fix first, not your rankings.
>
> (I build Alphaa, an AI agent for exactly this, so I'm biased.)
