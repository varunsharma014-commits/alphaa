## Quora (Varun, personal account)

### 6. How can I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?
- URL: https://www.quora.com/unanswered/How-can-I-get-my-business-recommended-by-ChatGPT
- Author: not shown (unanswered question, 1 follower)
- Context: no answers yet.
- Draft:
> ChatGPT can only recommend a business it can identify and verify, so start with the boring parts.
>
> 1. Make your name, address, phone number and hours identical everywhere: your website, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, industry directories. Conflicting details make an assistant less sure you are one real business.
> 2. Say plainly on your website what you do, who you do it for and where. One page per service, written the way a customer would ask about it.
> 3. Answer real customer questions on your site (pricing ranges, how long things take, what to expect). Assistants quote clear answers.
> 4. Earn reviews that mention the specific service and the town. "Great job" tells an AI nothing. "Replaced our water heater in Plano the same day" does.
> 5. Get mentioned in places other than your own site: local news, chamber listings, "best of" lists, community threads. Assistants lean on what others say about you.
> 6. Test it. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity the questions your customers would ask, a few times each, and note who gets named and which sources are cited. Then work on those sources.
>
> None of this is instant, and nobody can guarantee a recommendation. But a business with consistent details, specific reviews and clear answers is much easier for an AI to name.
>
> Disclosure: I build Alphaa, a tool that does this for local businesses, so I'm biased.

### 7. How can I get ChatGPT and other AI search engines to recommend my small business?
- URL: https://www.quora.com/unanswered/How-can-I-get-ChatGPT-and-other-AI-search-engines-to-recommend-my-small-business
- Author: not shown (unanswered question, 1 follower)
- Context: no answers yet.
- Draft:
> Think of it as making your business easy to verify rather than tricking an algorithm.
>
> AI assistants build their answer from what they can find and cross-check: your website, your Google Business Profile, reviews, directories and mentions on other sites. When those agree and are specific, you are a safe answer. When they are thin or contradict each other, the assistant names someone else.
>
> What I would do this week:
> - Search your own business name and fix every listing with an old phone number, address or hours.
> - Rewrite your homepage's first paragraph so it states the service, the customer and the area in one or two plain sentences.
> - Add a short questions section with the things customers really ask you, answered directly.
> - Ask your next few happy customers for a review that names the service they got.
> - Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity "who is the best [your service] in [your town]" and look at which sources they cite. Getting listed or mentioned in those sources is usually the fastest win.
>
> Each engine works a little differently and answers change from one run to the next, so check more than once and judge the trend, not a single answer.
>
> Disclosure: I build Alphaa, which checks and improves this for local businesses, so I'm biased.
