Espresso Beans: Your Questions, Answered

Which roast suits espresso at home?

Most home machines pull the sweetest shots from a medium-dark roast. Our Harbor Espresso blend is roasted for exactly that: chocolate and caramel, low acidity.

How fresh are your beans when they arrive?

We roast to order Monday to Thursday and ship the same day, so beans reach you within days of roasting. The roast date is printed on every bag.

Should I buy whole bean or ground?

Whole bean. Espresso needs a fine, even grind made right before brewing; pre-ground coffee loses aroma within hours.
