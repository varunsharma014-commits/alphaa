# Alphaa

> Alphaa is an AI marketing agent for small businesses. It runs the marketing every week, in the owner's own voice and brand colours: social media posts with branded images, Google Business Profile updates, blog posts that answer what customers ask, review replies, website fixes so AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity) and Google can recommend the business, and on the Growth plan Google and Meta ads and email campaigns. The owner can say "run my marketing for this week" by typing or out loud, approves anything public with one tap, or lets the agent run on its own. From $99 a month.

## Start

- [Get started](https://alphaa.app/start) (also on the homepage): enter your website and, in about 60 seconds, the agent asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity a real customer question about your business, in your website's language, shows who they named instead, and runs 23 checks on what AI can read on your site. If three or four of the AIs name you, it also asks two broader buyer questions and reports how many of the 12 answers named you. The score is measured from those answers and the checks, never estimated. With an email address, the agent then writes the business's first week of marketing (a Google update and two social posts with images in the business's own logo and colours, planned around that week's local news), one per email. No card needed.

## What the agent does

- Asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity the questions your customers ask, every week, and reports who got named — you or a competitor.
- Checks what AI can read on your site: crawler access, robots.txt, JavaScript-only pages, canonical tags, structured data (LocalBusiness schema), phone/address/hours, reviews, profile links, service pages, llms.txt.
- Writes and hosts the fixes: FAQ pages, structured data, llms.txt, fresh pages AI can quote.
- Keeps your Google Business Profile active with posts and review replies you approve.
- Sends one short plain-English note: what it did, what moved, what it needs a yes on.

## Integrations

Connect a website once and the agent publishes the changes you approve (or, on Pro, blog posts automatically via auto-publish). Every change has Undo.

- [All integrations](https://alphaa.app/integrations)
- [WordPress](https://alphaa.app/integrations/wordpress): free Alphaa Connector plugin. Publishes FAQ pages and blog posts, updates page titles and meta descriptions (works with Yoast and Rank Math), adds LocalBusiness structured data, llms.txt and AI-crawler robots.txt rules, and pings Bing via IndexNow.
- [Shopify](https://alphaa.app/integrations/shopify): one-click app with read_content and write_content only. Publishes blog posts and pages and updates their search titles and descriptions. It cannot edit theme files or robots.txt, and has no access to orders, customers or products.
- [Webflow](https://alphaa.app/integrations/webflow): one-click app with CMS read/write, Pages read/write and Sites read. Publishes blog posts to a CMS collection and updates page SEO titles and descriptions (live after the owner's next Webflow publish).
- [Wix](https://alphaa.app/integrations/wix): coming soon. Today the agent emails the owner's web person the exact change.
- Any other site: the agent emails the exact code and instructions to the owner's web person, or the Full Service team installs it.

## How it works

AI assistants answer from what they've indexed plus live web search. They favour businesses whose facts are clear, consistent and easy to cite across the web. Alphaa improves those public signals. It cannot edit an AI model directly, and it does not guarantee rankings or timelines.
